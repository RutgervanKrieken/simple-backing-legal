Privacy Policy for Simple Backing

Effective date: August 31, 2026

At Daybreak Studios ("we", "us") built Simple Backing as an app that runs entirely on your device. This policy explains what that means in practice.

We do not collect any data

Simple Backing does not have an account or login system, does not connect to the internet, and does not use analytics, advertising or crash-reporting services of any kind. We — the developer — have no access to your songs, audio files, notes, or any other content you put into the app.

Where your data lives

Everything you create in Simple Backing — bands, songs, setlists, notes, and the audio tracks you import — is stored locally in the app's storage on your iPad. Nothing is uploaded anywhere automatically.

Backups

The app lets you export a backup file containing your bands, songs and audio. This file is created locally and only leaves your device if you choose to share, save, or transfer it yourself (for example via AirDrop, Files, or iCloud Drive) — that transfer is under your control, not ours.

Permissions

Simple Backing does not request access to your microphone, camera, contacts, or location. It only reads the audio files you explicitly choose to import via the system file picker.

Children's privacy

Simple Backing does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children, because it does not collect information at all.

Changes to this policy

If this policy ever changes — for example, if a future version of the app adds a feature that involves data leaving the device — we'll update this page and the in-app version number accordingly.
