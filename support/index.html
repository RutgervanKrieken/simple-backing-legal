Frequently asked

Where is my data stored?

Everything you create in Simple Backing — bands, songs, setlists, notes, and imported audio — stays on your device. Nothing is uploaded automatically.

How do I back up or move my data to another iPad?

Open Settings → Export Backup to save a single file with your bands, songs, and audio. Use Settings → Import Backup on another device to restore it.

Multi-channel audio routing isn't working with my USB interface

Some interfaces report more channels than they can actually stream over USB. In Settings, try lowering "Requested channels," and make sure "Requested sample rate" matches your interface's actual clock rate.
